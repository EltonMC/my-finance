# Product Context for Design

Complete this before a product-sized visual feature. Keep it factual and revise it when product truth changes.

## Audience

An individual in Brazil managing everyday personal money from a phone.

## Primary user job

Quickly record, classify, and understand money in checking accounts and credit-card invoices before a payment or purchase is forgotten.

## Product context

Mobile-first responsive web used in brief, frequent sessions. Financial changes must state their account or card impact before confirmation. The first release is private per signed-in user, uses Brazilian Portuguese and BRL formatting, and has no bank synchronization.

## Voice

Clear, calm, direct.

## References and anti-references

- Reference: Mobills for the separation between accounts, transactions, cards, and invoices; do not copy its brand or layout.
- Anti-reference: spreadsheet-like desktop tables as the primary mobile experience.
- Anti-reference: celebratory or gamified language around spending.
