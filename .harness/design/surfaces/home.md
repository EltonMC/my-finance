# Home surface

## User job

See what needs attention today and begin a new account or card record without searching through dense data.

## Primary action

Open the quick-add menu, then choose account transaction, recurring bill payment, card purchase, installment, or chargeback.

## Hierarchy

Show pending bills first, then checking-account balances, card statement due amounts, and expected points. Keep each summary tappable to its detail area.

## Critical states

First-use onboarding, loading, no accounts or cards, overdue bill, paid statement, and unavailable network.
