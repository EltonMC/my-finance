# Card statement surface

## User job

Understand one card statement's closing date, due date, amount due, points estimate, and whether it can be paid.

## Primary action

Add a purchase, installment, or chargeback; for an unpaid closed statement, record a full payment from a checking account.

## Hierarchy

Statement status and amount due lead. Dates, payment action, point estimate, debits, and credits follow in that order.

## Critical states

Open statement, closed unpaid statement, paid statement, no card activity, insufficient configuration, and duplicate-payment prevention.
